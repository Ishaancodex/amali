# Motion audit (PRD §13) — Phase P11

Every interaction in PRD §13.2, where it lives, and how far it goes. **Done** matches the spec; **Partial** says what's missing; **Not built** says why.

All motion reads its numbers from `src/design/motion.ts`:

- **Tokens:** `dur`, `ease`, `spring`, `stagger`, plus the named variants. They are getters, so the presenter's **Motion speed** applies to everything:
  - **1×:** normal speed.
  - **0.5×:** durations double; springs keep their shape at half the frequency (stiffness ÷ 4, damping ÷ 2).
  - **0:** `MotionGlobalConfig.instantAnimations`, so every animation is instant.
- **Reduced motion:** `<MotionConfig reducedMotion="user">` in `Shell` drops transform animations, and the skeleton shimmer is off under `prefers-reduced-motion` (`globals.css`).
- **What animates:** only transform, opacity, `filter` (M15) and `clip-path` (M7), per PRD §13.3.
