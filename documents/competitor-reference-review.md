# Competitor reference review — "Ámali Island · Resident App"

## Context

Stakeholders prefer the look and feel of a competing pitch (`reference/Amali-Island-Resident-App.html`, a 3.2 MB single-file prototype). The ask: understand its design, animation and interaction, write down observations, and say whether we can do the same in our product. This plan is the review. On approval I'll save it as `documents/competitor-reference-review.md` (the "note down" deliverable). **No app code changes are proposed until the stakeholder decisions at the end are made.**

I read the file with base64 fonts/images stripped (157 KB of real CSS/JS/markup). I did not run it in a browser, so every timing and effect below comes from the source.

## What it is (and isn't)

- Vanilla JS + hand-written CSS in one HTML file. One persona (resident "Layla", Villa 7), all data hard-coded, slot availability from a seeded hash. Staff personas are present but disabled ("Next phase").
- 14 screens: Home, Amenities, Reserve a space, Pass, Bookings, What's On, Kids' Club, Dining (with cart + checkout), Wallet, My Unit, Search, Activity, Profile, SOS. Plus EN/ع switching.
- It is a **visual/interaction layer only**: no booking rules, no waitlists, no multi-device sync, no RBAC. Our product has all of that. Only the surface is comparable.
- Content is "Ámali Island" (marina, pontoon, yachts, villas). Ours is Amali Residences (padel, screening, creche…). Same brand, different community.

## Observations

### Design language

- **Palette is the brand book's, used with discipline:** Ember `#0E0D0C`, Pearl `#F2EDE3`, Sand `#BF8063` as the _only_ accent, Stone for muted. Teal `#2C7884` is reserved for "available now" (status dot, "confirmed" tag). Their swatch strip also lists "Residences Teal / Mist": relevant to us, since ours is the Residences product.
- **Four typefaces with strict roles:** Julius Sans One (wide, uppercase, 0.2–0.3em tracking) for every label/eyebrow/button/tab; **Instrument Serif italic** for titles, big numerals (date "19", balance "2,450", stepper count) and emphasis; Barlow for body; a script face for the resident's name only, once, on Home. The serif-italic-plus-wide-caps pairing is what reads as "luxury" and is the biggest visual difference from ours (Poppins + Barlow + Alex Brush).
- **Dark top, light bottom:** every screen opens with an Ember header (sand radial glow top-right, faint teal bottom-left, grain overlay) and the Pearl content slides over it as a rounded sheet (`border-radius:26px 26px 0 0; margin-top:-24px`). Photo screens use the render with a gradient fade to Ember so text always sits on a dark band, never on the image.
- **Hairlines, not shadows:** cards are `inset 0 0 0 1px` rings on Pearl-2, 18–24px radii, generous spacing, 1px dividers. The only real shadows are on the floating tab bar, ticket, and wallet card.
- **Texture:** an SVG `feTurbulence` noise layer (`mix-blend-mode:soft-light`, 22–42% opacity) over dark headers and gradient tiles gives a paper/film grain.
- **Art tiles for things without photos:** 11 named gradients (sand, teal, cinema, dusk, blush…) with a thin white line motif (racket, waves, arch, strings, glass…). Events and the Kids' Club use these instead of a grey placeholder.
- **Signature components:** (1) the _next booking_ as a **ticket stub** (Ember card, dashed perforation, two notches cut out with Pearl circles, "Pass" on the stub); (2) the **Pass** screen as a keepsake: Pearl ticket on Ember, wordmark, 6 fields, sand-dashed perforation, QR, "Tonight" tag; (3) a **floating pill tab bar** (inset 12px, 66px tall, 88% Ember + 20px backdrop blur, a 4px sand dot above the active tab, hides on pushed screens); (4) a **sticky blurred CTA** whose eyebrow names the chosen slot ("Fri 23 Oct · 20:00") and stays disabled until one is picked; (5) a "live today" rail of small status cards (teal dot + "3 berths free").
- **Detail rules worth noting:** taken slots stay visible, struck through, so busyness is legible; "Tonight" is the only sand tag on the Bookings page; unread = tiny sand dot; empty states are italic-serif one-liners with one pill action.

### Animation (all from source)

- **One easing curve everywhere:** `cubic-bezier(.22,.8,.24,1)`, a long soft ease-out. Durations are 0.25–0.7s; nothing bounces.
- **Push/pop:** new screen slides in 0.46s while the one beneath drifts back 24% and dims to 85% brightness; pop is 0.38s. Our Navigator already pushes at 100% and drifts the under-screen 30% to 0.9 opacity, so this is close.
- **Shared-element morph** (the showpiece): tapping an amenity row morphs its thumbnail into the full-width detail hero (View Transitions API, `view-transition-name:hero`, 0.6s) and morphs back on pop; falls back to a plain push where unsupported or under reduced motion.
- **Ken Burns:** hero and header photos ease `scale(1.07 → 1)` over 2.2–2.6s on arrival (skipped on the morph arrival).
- **Staggered rise:** body blocks fade up 16px, 0.7s, 50ms apart (up to ~8), plus hero text at 0.12s.
- **"Lit" status bar:** once content scrolls past the hero, the status bar gets a 14px frosted Pearl background and flips to ink text.
- **Sheets:** slide up 0.42s over a 50% scrim with 2px blur. Toast drops from the top (~0.3s), auto-dismisses at 2.2s.
- **Micro:** button press `scale(.98)`, tile `scale(.97)`, toggle thumb 0.25s, nav item underline/padding shift on the index.
- **Hold-to-confirm SOS:** a conic-gradient ring fills over exactly 2s via `requestAnimationFrame` writing `--p`; release resets it; Space/Enter works for keyboard. Then the sheet becomes "help is on the way" with a named responder.
- **Reduced motion:** a blanket `prefers-reduced-motion` rule collapses all durations.
- **Dead code:** a full-screen WebGL fbm "water" shader is written but **never attached** (`water.attach` is never called). The hero is a still image with Ken Burns. Don't price a shader into this.

### Interactions

- Booking on **one screen**: 7-day strip, 4-column slot grid, guest stepper, house notes, sticky Reserve. Reserve pushes the Pass screen (a toast says "Reserved. Your pass is ready"), and the booking appears in Bookings and Activity.
- Cancel is a bottom sheet that says the slot is "released to other residents straight away". Dining has a real cart, delivery/collect toggle, wallet checkout, and an Activity entry. Wallet top-up is a confirm sheet. Search is live-filtered with grouped results and sand `<mark>` highlights; recent searches are chips.
- Scroll position is saved per stack entry and restored on back.
- **Arabic:** `dir="rtl"` flips the whole screen; animations use a `--dir` variable so pushes mirror; italics and letter-spacing are switched off in Arabic; Amiri/IBM Plex Arabic replace the Latin faces; chevrons flip.
- Presenter aids: left index of screens (deep-linkable via `#hash`), right-hand "Design notes" panel that changes per screen, restart button.

## Risks and constraints

1. **Accessibility:** their micro-type and Sand-on-Pearl contrast would regress our P11 AA work. Mitigated as noted above.
2. **Rules in CLAUDE.md:** no raw colours/sizes/durations in components: every value above must land in `design/tokens.ts` / `design/motion.ts` first, and `pnpm check:tokens` must pass. Files < ~250 lines → split into `parts/`.
3. **Perf in dual view:** blur + blend-mode + big images, ×2 devices, scaled. Needs a frame-timing check (`PERF=1`).
4. **Scale of the re-skin:** touches ~8 resident screens + `ui/*` primitives; staff devices are unaffected (already dark).
5. **IP/provenance:** reimplement from observation only. Don't copy their code, SVG motifs, copy text, or embedded renders. Their renders are Amali brand assets; confirm with the Amali team what we may use for the Residences community.
6. **Brand authority:** their serif italic and Julius Sans One aren't from the brand book (Aviano Sans / DIN 2014 / "Romantically" script). A stakeholder decision, not an engineering one.

## Proposed adoption order (only after you say go)

1. **Foundations:** ember tokens, fonts, grain, `HeaderBand`/`BodySheet`, glass tab bar, ticket-stub card, art tiles → restyle Home + Amenities list/detail first.
2. **Motion:** Ken Burns, rise stagger, status-bar frost, push tweak; then the `layoutId` shared-image morph (spike → keep or drop).
3. **Signature moments:** Pass keepsake wired to real bookings/QR; hold-to-confirm SOS wired to a Guard notification.
4. **Sweep:** remaining screens, Arabic rules, `motion-audit.md` update, axe + e2e + perf; log in `trd.md` §16.

## Decisions taken

1. Adopt the serif-italic + wide-caps type pairing (Instrument Serif, Julius Sans One).
2. Pilot first on Home, Amenities list/detail, Pass and SOS; sweep the other resident screens afterwards.
